02 · Framework

AI should adapt to the human goal, not only to the prompt.

The framework moves through four layers: intent, interaction, growth and outcome.

Layer 01

Intent

Is the person trying to learn, decide, create, explore, solve or reflect?

Layer 02

Interaction

The AI chooses a dialogue structure that supports the user’s actual cognitive goal.

Layer 03

Growth

The experience is evaluated by understanding, judgment, creativity and independence.

03 · Interaction Patterns

Ten patterns for AI that supports thinking.

These patterns are a design hypothesis, not a finished theory. They are intended to be tested, challenged and developed.

01

Intent-Aware Interaction

Identify whether the person wants speed, learning, reflection, exploration or a decision before choosing the response model.

02

Reflection Loop

Invite the user to form an initial position before the system supplies a complete answer.

03

Cognitive Friction

Add small, deliberate moments of effort when removing all effort would weaken understanding.

04

Thinking Scaffolding

Break complex reasoning into questions, clues, comparisons and manageable cognitive steps.

05

Multiple Perspectives

Present several credible interpretations instead of disguising one answer as the only possible truth.

06

Confidence Transparency

Show uncertainty, assumptions and the limits of the system’s knowledge.

07

Human Checkpoint

Return value-based, consequential and irreversible decisions to the human.

08

Creative Divergence

Expand the space of possibilities before converging on a single polished solution.

09

Growth Memory

Remember how the person’s abilities are developing, not only their preferences and previous requests.

10

Wisdom Delay

Delay a complete answer when a brief pause, question or hypothesis could produce deeper judgment.

05 · Concept Product

ThinkLab is not a chatbot.

It is a thinking environment that changes its behaviour according to what the person is trying to achieve.

ThinkLab / Prototype 01 Human-centered mode

Learning mode

What would you like to understand more deeply?

ThinkLab will first identify what you already know, then guide you through questions, comparisons and small experiments.

The best AI is not the one that thinks instead of us. It is the one that leaves us more curious, more capable and more human.

Better answers are not enough.
